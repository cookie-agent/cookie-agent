Notes

  • Heat is the share of the TTL left: hot above ½, warm from ⅕ to ½, cooling under ⅕, then "cache expired". The countdown counts minutes (hours above 90 min: 11h), and seconds in its last minute.
  • No state by colour alone: the flame or snowflake and the countdown carry it; colour only grades the heat (orange, faded orange, amber).
  • The dot marks a compaction planned before expiry; the tooltip and the card say when, and for which scheduled prompt.
  • "~" marks a TTL cookie assumes (fallback_ttl, the chatgpt provider's 30 min, an ACP agent's cache_ttl) rather than one it set on the request.
  • Where the numbers come from: the daemon sends a small cache clock with the session view: last request, TTL and its source, the compaction due time, and the next fire. Clients only count down, with no rules of their own.
  • Shown for the main session only; a subagent's badge appears when you open the subagent.
