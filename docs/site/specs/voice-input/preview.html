Voice input: the composer with a microphone

2026-11-21 · draft for docs/site/specs/voice-input.md · the page is the real web client (installed build); the microphone parts are a mock-up

Try it

Both frames work: click the microphone (or press Ctrl+Shift+Space in the composer). Nothing is recorded; the level meter and the words are made up.

  1. The first time, the browser asks for the microphone.
  2. Speak. ✓ or Enter stops; × or Esc cancels.
  3. “Transcribing…”, then the words land at the end of the draft. Record again to add more.
